Built by someone who's been there

In my twenties, what started as general anxiety became panic attacks and gradually consumed my life. For two years I struggled to go to restaurants, social events, couldn’t ride in cars or get on planes. My personal and professional life suffered. I was in a constant state of fight or flight. Every day was a minute-by-minute battle. I felt unable to convey to others what I was going through, and the truth was I didn’t really know the answer myself, and feared I would never recover.

Through cognitive behavioral therapy and exposure techniques, I learned the facts about what was actually happening to me and began to challenge the panic head on. Understanding the science behind panic and implementing the tools I learned to fight back is what ultimately freed me.

I built Don’t Panic to put those same tools—the ones that gave me my life back—into your pocket for the moment you need them most. You’re not broken and I’m living proof you can find freedom from the grip of panic and anxiety.

I recovered. I found balance. I got my life back. I want to help you get there too.

— Brandon, Founder of Don't Panic
