2. Description of Service

Don’t Panic is a general-wellness application that provides self-help tools informed by cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and mindfulness research. Features include:

  • An 8-phase guided panic relief system
  • Breathing exercises with visual and haptic guidance
  • Grounding techniques
  • Guided meditations
  • Educational lessons on panic and anxiety
  • Guided journaling with mood tracking
  • Goal setting and progress analytics
  • Calming games and trivia
  • Affirmations and panic facts
  • Exposure hierarchy (Fear Ladder) tracker
  • Apple Watch companion app

Feature availability depends on whether you are using the free tier or a premium subscription.
